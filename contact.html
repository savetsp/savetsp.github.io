Contact public leaders

NYC Parks offered to take the calisthenics park out of its project. Tell our leaders to hold it to that.

1. Post a video and tag the Mayor

Film 30 seconds at the bars or courts, on Instagram or TikTok. Say who you are and why this place matters. The Mayor is @nycmayor on both, so tag him. On Instagram, also tag @save.tompkins. Use #SaveTSP #SaveTompkins.

2. Send email

It goes to Parks Commissioner Tricia Shimamura and Manhattan Borough President Brad Hoylman-Sigal.

Email now

Opens your email app with the message ready. Add your name, and a sentence of your own if you'd like. A copy goes to savetsp@gmail.com, so we can keep you posted.

3. Contact city leaders

The Mayor and the Comptroller take messages by web form. Copy the message, then paste it in. On the Mayor's form, ask them to share it with Deputy Mayor for Operations Julia Kerson, who oversees Parks.
