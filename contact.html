Email city leaders

This Tue, Nov 24, Community Board 3 votes on whether to demolish the calisthenics area at Tompkins Square Park and close it for two years. Tell them what you think.

We add them to the end of your message. Officials pay more attention to signed messages from constituents.

Email now

Opens your email app with the message ready. You can edit it there before sending. A copy goes to savetsp@gmail.com, the campaign’s address, so we can keep you posted.

Want to change the message first?

What we want to say

Edit the message as you like. Adding a sentence about how you use the park makes it stronger.

To

Email now

"Email now" opens your email app with the message filled in. If you use email in a browser, such as Gmail, tap "Copy message" and paste it into a new email. The addresses are included.

Who gets it

  • Community Board 3. The full board votes on the plan on Nov 24.
  • Parks Commissioner Tricia Shimamura. Heads the agency that wrote the plan.
  • Borough President Brad Hoylman-Sigal. Appoints the members of Community Board 3.

Also write to the Mayor and the Comptroller

They take messages by web form. Copy the message, then paste it in. On the Mayor's form, ask them to share it with Deputy Mayor for Operations Julia Kerson, who oversees Parks.
