Who he is

Harvey Epstein is the New York City Council member for District 2, which includes the East Village and Tompkins Square Park.

Why we are writing to him

On May 16, at a Parks session in the park, he "spoke of his commitment to advocating for the wishes of the people," according to Parks' own minutes. The wishes of the people were clear: keep the courts and the fitness area untouched. On October 27, he and Assembly Member Powers asked Parks to keep the existing equipment and add accessible equipment, in a modified Option 3. Let's thank him, and ask him to see it through.
