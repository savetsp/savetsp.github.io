Katie and Franco at the Tompkins bars

Should Parks demolish Tompkins to make it “accessible”?? Ask Katie. Most mornings, she brings Franco, who just turned 5 months old, and rolls his stroller right next to whatever part of the equipment she is using. On any given day you’ll see parents, kids, seniors and serious athletes training side by side, for free, in an inclusive public space.

Still, Parks is adamant about demolishing the bars and rebuilding the whole area, closing it for an extended period, for no defensible reason. The community has a much better plan. Parks can add an ADA-compliant rubber transition ramp now, keep the bars, and add adaptive equipment right next to them, if needed, and as it already did at Columbus Park. The area becomes compliant right away, stays open, and costs a fraction of the price.

CB3 votes on Tuesday, Nov 24, and city leaders should read the community plan and respond. You can read it too.
