Katie and Franco at the Tompkins bars

Katie works out at the bars every morning with her baby Franco, 5 months old, parking his stroller right beside her. “I can’t bring him to a gym, so we can come out here.”

Parks wants to demolish the bars and rebuild the whole area, closing it for an extended period. The community has a better plan: fix the steep rubber tiles now with an accessible ramp, keep the bars, and add adaptive equipment right next to them. The area becomes accessible right away, stays open, and costs a fraction of the price.
