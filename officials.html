Email our other leaders

Besides Council Member Harvey Epstein, five more leaders have a say. CB3 votes on October 27. They can tell Parks and the board, before the vote, that this plan is wrong for the community. It takes two minutes.

1. Edit the message

We add the greeting for each official. Adding a sentence about how you use the park makes it stronger.

Fastest way. One email to Assembly Member Powers and Senator Kavanagh at once.

Email both

2. Send it to each official

The Borough President, the Mayor and the Governor only take messages through a web form. Tap "Copy and open form", then paste the message into the form.

  • Keith Powers

    State Assembly Member, District 74

    Represents the East Village in Albany. Most of the money comes from a state grant for swimming pools.

    Email or call 212-979-9696

  • Brian Kavanagh

    State Senator, District 27

    Represents Manhattan below 14th Street in the State Senate.

    Email or call 212-298-5565

  • Brad Hoylman-Sigal

    Manhattan Borough President

    Appoints the members of Community Board 3.

    Copy and open form or call 212-669-8300

  • Zohran Mamdani

    Mayor of New York City

    NYC Parks reports to the Mayor. The state grant can only go to the pool, so the Mayor's office added $3.83 million of city money "because we can't just do a pool," as Parks told CB3. A city that is more affordable, equal and inclusive does not close a free outdoor gym for two years.

    Copy and open form or call 311

  • Kathy Hochul

    Governor of New York

    $6.1 million of this project comes from the Governor's NY SWIMS grant, a program for pools. It should go to the pool.

    Copy and open form or call 518-474-8390

"Email" opens your email app with the message filled in. If you use email in a browser, such as Gmail, tap "Copy message" and paste it into a new email.
